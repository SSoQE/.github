## 🌱 About SSoQE

The Science School on Quantitative Ecology (SSoQE) is an intensive school for Master's and PhD students in ecology and related fields. Organized jointly by Charles University in Prague and the University of Bayreuth, it combines ecological questions with modern data analysis, reproducible research, and collaborative scientific practice.

SSoQE has run since 2024 and alternates between locations in Czechia and Germany. The 2026 school takes place in Wallenfels, Germany.

## 🎯 What participants work on

- framing ecological questions for exploration, inference, and prediction;
- reproducible R projects, analytical pipelines, Git, and GitHub collaboration;
- species distribution modelling, biodiversity patterns, community data, and ordination;
- sampling standardisation, null models, and bias in ecological and paleoecological data;
- fossil pollen, phylogenetics, macroevolution, deep learning, and AI-supported programming.

Teaching materials are maintained in independent repositories by the respective lecturers. Availability can differ between repositories, so use the programme and links provided during the school rather than assuming that every organization repository is a current lesson.

## 👥 Organizers

SSoQE is organized by researchers from Charles University in Prague and the University of Bayreuth, together with invited contributors. See the [team page](https://ssoqe.github.io/SSoQE_website/About/team.html) for current organizers and lecturers.
